## K. Conduct: Proof Economy Code of Ethics

*Ethics, fair dealing, and competitive conduct are governed by the Proof Economy Code of Ethics, under the Proof Economy Standards Alliance (PESA). The Linux Foundation and LF Decentralized Trust set no ethics or fair dealing obligations beyond their Code of Conduct and Antitrust Policy; this section adds to those and replaces neither.*

**K.1 Condition.** Your rights under every license in the family are conditioned on your compliance with the Proof Economy Code of Ethics (the **Code**), governed by PESA and published at https://github.com/proofprotocol/CODE-OF-ETHICS and https://proofeconomy.foundation, in the version in effect when you first used the Covered Work (version 1.0 at the date of this section) or any later version you elect in writing. This section is the licensing term by which the Code binds you, as Code Section 1 contemplates.

**K.2 Later provisions.** Fair dealing and non-harmful competitive practices provisions, and any other provisions PESA adds to the Code after the version that binds you, apply to you only once you elect them. K.3 applies to you in every case.

**K.3 Minimum duties.** Whatever the Code version, you must not: (a) present verification or validation as proof, or label a claim above its true assurance level; (b) make a public claim under a Specification without citing the Record ID or Registry Receipt that supports it; (c) conceal your funding tier or a financial interest in the outcome of a run you perform, witness, adjudicate, or publish; (d) test, witness, or adjudicate your own control, or one in which you hold a financial interest, except as a Specification expressly permits with declared separation; (e) sabotage, poison, or degrade another party's run, corpus, witness, or records, or tune a control to recognize the test rather than the behavior under test; or (f) use a Covered Work, a conformance claim, or a PESA mark to exclude a competitor for any reason other than a proven breach.

**K.4 Delegated authority.** The Licensor delegates to PESA, as the governing body applying the Code under its Section 10, the authority to determine breach of K.1 or K.3 and to suspend or terminate your rights under this section. The Licensor agrees to be bound by those determinations. The Licensor and any party with a commercial stake in the matter take no part in deciding it.

**K.5 Notice and cure.** You will receive written notice of a breach and 30 days to cure before any termination, as in G.2. Cure of a first breach reinstates your rights automatically. A breach PESA's published enforcement procedures class as Critical (fabrication or alteration of proof records, self-certification, unauthorized testing, sabotage, or collusion) carries no cure right; PESA may suspend your rights at once pending a hearing, and the suspension lapses if no determination issues within 60 days.

**K.6 Effect.** On termination under this section, every right granted to you under the family ends, including any patent license under Part P and any right to use names and marks under G.4, and your conformance claims are withdrawn and recorded as revoked in the Registry. G.3 applies. Anyone who received a Covered Work from you keeps their rights while they comply. Rights already granted under Part S in specification text are not affected (S.5).

**K.7 Appeal and courts.** You may appeal a PESA determination under the appeal procedures Code Section 10 requires. Nothing in this section limits G.10 or the jurisdiction of any court.

**K.8 Competition law.** Nothing in this section limits your freedom to build, sell, price, or use any product, or to compete in any market. This section applies subject to applicable antitrust and competition law.
